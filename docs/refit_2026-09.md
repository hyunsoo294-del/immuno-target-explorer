# Refit 2026-09 — HER2 × 4-1BB × Jurkat NF-κB

This note is the history for the next refit. The score remains a panel-selection heuristic.

## Three defects

1. F1 was saturated. `ABC50_base` was 8,000, so a Jurkat ABC50 sat near 16,000. Every HER2-amplified line scored 99.9–100 and an 80-fold density difference was invisible.
2. The floor was too high. Heterogeneity (imputed), checkpoint (gated to a constant 100), and epitope proximity (a TAA constant) added the same offset to every line. MDA-MB-231 scored about 68% of the top line while measuring 3.6% of it. Measured dynamic range was 27.8×. The model range was about 1.5×.
3. Adhesion was the only factor that tracked Emax among antigen-positive lines, and it ignored culture accessibility. SK-BR-3 and NCI-N87 grow in clumps. DepMap `GrowthPattern` calls both them and Calu-3 adherent, so it cannot see the difference.

## What changed

- F1 is a multiplicative gate. `ABC50_base` is 30,000. Effective ABC50 is that value times the effector multiplier (Jurkat NF-κB → 60,000). Above the threshold, density does not raise or lower the score.
- A factor the effector gates off has weight 0. It is shown as an em dash, not as 100.
- Adhesion is the RNA rank times synapse competence times a hand-entered accessibility. The five panel lines are in `data/curated/morphology.yaml`. Other lines use GrowthPattern and are flagged as estimated.
- Only `4-1BB__Jurkat_NFkB` uses the fitted weights (contact 40, internalization 24, glycocalyx 20, heterogeneity 9, epitope 7, checkpoint 0). Other combinations keep their previous relative weights after F1 is removed and a gated checkpoint is set to 0. They are marked `not fitted`.

## Unresolved confound

In this panel, HER2 density and contact morphology point in opposite directions. The gate does not fit "high density lowers Emax". It also does not claim that Calu-3 wins only because it is flat. Those two stories cannot be separated with these five lines. Do not add a bell-shaped or descending F1 until a panel breaks the confound.

Antigen depletion is not a candidate explanation. Even the highest expressor presents about 0.4 nM of binding sites at typical plating density, far below the top concentrations of the dose-response.
