# Refit 2026-09 — HER2 × 4-1BB × Jurkat NF-κB

This note is the history for the next refit. The score remains a panel-selection heuristic.

Full dose-response fits, raw RLU, not single-concentration reads. Combination: 4-1BB arm, Jurkat NF-κB reporter.

Raw RLU is sensitive to plating density and target-cell growth. Later fold-over-background values should be extra rows, not replacements.

## Three defects

1. F1 was saturated. `ABC50_base` was 8,000, so a Jurkat ABC50 sat near 16,000. Every HER2-amplified line scored 99.9–100 and an 80-fold density difference was invisible.
2. The floor was too high. Heterogeneity (imputed), checkpoint (gated to a constant 100), and epitope proximity (a TAA constant) added the same offset to every line. MDA-MB-231 scored about 68% of the top line while measuring 3.6% of it. Measured dynamic range was 27.8×. The model range was about 1.5×.
3. Adhesion was the only factor that tracked Emax among antigen-positive lines, and it ignored culture accessibility. SK-BR-3 and NCI-N87 grow in clumps. DepMap `GrowthPattern` calls both them and Calu-3 adherent, so it cannot see the difference.

## What changed

- F1 is a multiplicative gate. `ABC50_base` is 30,000. Effective ABC50 is that value times the effector multiplier (Jurkat NF-κB → 60,000). Above the threshold, density does not raise or lower the score.
- A factor the effector gates off has weight 0. It is shown as an em dash, not as 100.
- Adhesion is the RNA rank times synapse competence times a hand-entered accessibility. The five panel lines are in `data/curated/morphology.yaml`. Other lines use GrowthPattern and are flagged as estimated.
- Only `4-1BB__Jurkat_NFkB` uses the fitted weights (contact 40, internalization 24, glycocalyx 20, heterogeneity 9, epitope 7, checkpoint 0). Other combinations keep their previous relative weights after F1 is removed and a gated checkpoint is set to 0. They are marked `not fitted`.

## Unresolved confound

In this panel, HER2 density and contact morphology point in opposite directions. The gate does not fit "high density lowers Emax". It also does not claim that Calu-3 wins only because it is flat. Those two stories cannot be separated with these five lines. Do not add a bell-shaped or descending F1 until a panel breaks the confound.

Antigen depletion is not a candidate explanation. Even the highest expressor presents about 0.4 nM of binding sites at typical plating density, far below the top concentrations of the dose-response.

## v3 — floor and calibration

The v2 rank matched measured Emax. The magnitudes did not. Calu-3 / HCC1954 was 1.67× measured and about 1.05× in score. A weighted sum of 0–100 sub-scores cannot exceed its largest input ratio, so the weights were not the lever.

Two changes, both still on the v2 sub-scores:

1. A factor whose sub-score SD across the result set is below `variance_eps` (1.0) is removed from the weighted sum. The remaining weights are renormalized to 100. The value stays on screen, greyed, with flag `무변별인자`. On this HER2 Jurkat panel that drops 균일성 and 에피톱. Effective weights: 접촉 47.6, 내재화 28.6, 당질층 23.8.
2. `log10(Emax) = a + b × score` is fit by OLS on the five measured rows for this combination only. n < 4 is refused. The fit is monotone and is not used to re-rank. The 95% prediction interval is shown because n=5 is wide. A score outside the fitted score range is flagged `외삽`.

Scores after step 1, then the step-2 fit. HCC1954 is 35.02, which displays as 35.0. A hand product of the one-decimal sub-scores lands on 35.1; the scorer keeps full precision.

`log10(Emax) = 2.8156 + 0.03776 × score`, n=5, R²=0.974, fit date 2026-09-30. Calu-3 / HCC1954 predicted is 1.43×. The remaining gap to 1.67× is inside this n and is not chased.

Cohort logistic replacement of the rank/z-score sub-scores is not in this pass. That change moves the sub-scores, so this calibration would be refit afterwards rather than reused.

## Guardrails

- Do not steepen accessibility to close the ratio gap. The five values stay 1.00 / 0.80 / 0.60 / 0.55 / 0.90. Moving them to 1.00 / 0.60 / 0.35 / 0.30 until the measured ranks fit is reverse-engineering.
- Do not apply `score ** k`. It has no mechanism and amplifies noise the same way as signal.
- Do not fit a bell-shaped or descending F1. Density and contact morphology still point in opposite directions on these five lines.
- Do not copy the `4-1BB__Jurkat_NFkB` weights, or this calibration, onto any other combination.
- A later weight change needs a fresh `validate_model.py` run and a `docs/refit_<date>.md` entry that says which measurements justified it.

Cell diameter is a field only. `mean_diameter_um` is null, and `use_cell_size` is false, so `accessibility × (d / d_ref)²` is not in the score.
